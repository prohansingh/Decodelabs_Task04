KEY INSIGHTS

1. Electronics products generated the highest overall sales revenue.

2. Online payment methods were used more frequently by customers.

3. Sales showed noticeable growth during peak shopping periods.

4. Referral platforms such as Instagram and Google contributed significantly to customer orders.

5. Most orders were successfully delivered, while a smaller percentage of orders were cancelled or returned.

--------------------------------------------------

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Increase marketing efforts on high-performing referral platforms.

2. Improve customer service to reduce cancellations and returns.

3. Focus inventory planning on high-demand products.

4. Launch promotional campaigns during high-sales months.

5. Enhance customer retention strategies through personalized offers.
